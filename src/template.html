Aerospace Flight Game

Pick an aircraft and fly through all 12 rings as fast as you can. Each plane uses its own lift, drag and thrust numbers, so they all handle differently.

Press 1 to 5 to pick a plane. Keep your speed up: if you pull the nose too high, the wing stalls and stops making lift.
